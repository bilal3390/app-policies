Privacy Policy

EMI Calculator (Android) · Effective 9 November 2026 · Developer: HumanOp

EMI Calculator ("the app") helps you calculate loan payments, compare loans and plan prepayments. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information you enter

Loan amounts, interest rates, tenures, names you give to saved loans and your currency preference are stored only on your device. We do not have access to them and they are never uploaded to our servers. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use device information to serve ads, including:

  • the Android Advertising ID (a resettable identifier),
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • device type, operating system version and app usage data related to ad delivery (impressions, clicks).

How Google uses this information is described in Google's advertising policy and Google's privacy policy. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and certain US states) the app shows a consent dialog before any ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the app.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Settings → Google → Ads.

Analytics and crash reports

The app does not include a separate analytics SDK. Aggregated, non-identifying crash and performance statistics may be collected by Google Play as part of the normal operation of the Play Store.

Permissions

The app requests network access (to load ads) and the advertising ID permission. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or files.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Other than the ad-serving data described above, which goes directly from your device to Google, no information is shared with third parties.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because the app stores your inputs only on your device, you can exercise these rights by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. For ad-related data, use the in-app privacy options or your device's advertising settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will change and material updates will be noted in the app's store listing.
